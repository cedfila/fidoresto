Delete my FidOresto account

You can delete your FidOresto account and its data at any time, in two ways.

1. From the app

Open FidOresto → Profile → Delete my account, then confirm with your password. The account is deleted straight away.

2. By email (without the app)

Send an email from your account's email address, with the subject "FidOresto account deletion".

Request deletion

We process requests within 30 days at the latest and confirm the deletion by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email, name, nickname, phone, delivery address)
  • Your loyalty cards, points, history and orders
  • For a business: its businesses, logos, menus, offers and loyalty programmes

What is kept

  • Feedback and problem reports sent from the app, with no link to your identity
