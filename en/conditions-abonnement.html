Free trial of the Max plan

  • Every new business account gets 3 months of the Max plan free (90 days from the date the account is created), once only.
  • No payment method is required during the trial. It never turns into a paid subscription on its own.
  • You are notified by app notification and by email 7 days before the end of the trial, then the day before.
  • At the end of the trial, if you have not subscribed, your account automatically moves to the Free plan.
  • This trial offer is for a limited time and may be withdrawn for new sign-ups at any time. Trials already started run until the end.

Subscribing and payment

  • You subscribe to Max in the app. Payment is taken and managed by Google Play (Android) or by Apple's App Store (iPhone), using the payment method on your Google or Apple account, when you confirm the purchase.
  • FidOresto never receives or stores any bank card details.
  • The subscription is linked to your FidOresto account: it works on both Android and iPhone.

Renewal and cancellation

  • The subscription is monthly with no commitment. It renews automatically each month unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • To cancel:
    • Android: Google Play app → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → FidOresto → Cancel;
    • iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → FidOresto → Cancel Subscription.
  • After cancelling, you keep the Max plan until the end of the month already paid, then your account moves to the Free plan.
  • Deleting the app or your FidOresto account does not cancel the subscription: remember to cancel it in Google Play or the App Store.

Refunds

As payments are collected by Google or Apple, refund requests follow their rules and are made to them (Google Play: "Request a refund" in your order history; Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com). If you have any difficulty, write to us.

Declined payment

If the renewal payment cannot be taken, you keep the Max plan for a 7-day grace period, giving you time to update your payment method. After that, your account moves to the Free plan.

Moving to the Free plan

  • Nothing is deleted: your customers, their points, your rewards, menus, offers and businesses are kept.
  • Max-only features are simply paused. Only your first business stays visible on the map.
  • Orders already accepted can be completed. New orders are no longer possible.
  • If you subscribe again, everything comes back as it was.

Inactive accounts (Free plan)

  • A business account on the Free plan with no sign-in and no validated customer visit for 3 months is hidden from the map. You are warned by email one week before. It reappears as soon as you sign in again.
  • After 2 years with no activity at all, the account and its data may be deleted. You are warned twice by email beforehand.
  • An account subscribed to Max is never hidden for inactivity.

Changes to prices and terms

If the price changes, you will be told in advance. Google Play and the App Store ask for your consent according to their own rules; without your consent, the subscription ends at the end of the current period. These terms may change: the date at the top of the page shows the latest version.
