Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This English version is provided for convenience. If there is any difference, the French version prevails.

FidOresto is a loyalty app for many local shops. This page explains what data we collect, why, who it is shared with and how to delete it. We never sell any data and we show no advertising.

Data controller

Cédric Filain, publisher of the FidOresto app, Saint-Paul, La Réunion (France).
Contact: fidorestocontact@gmail.com

Data collected and how it is used

DataWhy
Email address, password (encrypted), name and nickname, account type (customer or business)To create and secure your account, sign you in and recover your password
Phone number and delivery address (optional)Sent to the shop only when you place an order, or if you agree to share your contact details with the shops where you are a customer
Loyalty points, visit history and rewardsTo run the loyalty cards
Orders (items, pickup time, comment)To send your order to the shop and track its status
For businesses: business name and category, address, position on the map, phone, opening hours, logo, menu photos, offersTo show the business to customers in the app
Referral: the shop whose poster you scanned or whose code you entered when signing upTo add its loyalty card automatically and tell the shop how many customers its poster has brought in (without your identity)

For businesses: plan (Free or Max), free trial dates, subscription status and purchase identifier provided by Google Play or the App StoreTo turn on Max features, warn you before the trial ends and check that the subscription has been paid. Date the app was last used (and, for businesses, the date of the last validated customer visit)To identify inactive accounts (see "How long we keep data")
Feedback and problem reports sent from the app (message, optional screenshot, phone model, app version)To fix problems and improve the app

Your location

Your phone's location is only used to show nearby shops and their distance. It is used on your phone and is not stored on our servers. You can refuse access to your location: the app works without it.

Camera and photos

The camera is used to scan loyalty QR codes (businesses) and, if you wish, to photograph a menu or choose a logo. Only the images you choose to send are uploaded.

Service providers

  • Supabase: hosting of the database, accounts and images.
  • Brevo: sending emails (confirmation, forgotten password, internal notifications).
  • Google Maps and OpenStreetMap: displaying the map and looking up a business's address.
  • Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: sending notifications to phones.
  • Google Play and Apple's App Store: distributing the app and handling business subscription payments.
  • Umami: anonymous audience measurement for the fidoresto.fr website (pages viewed, country, device type, clicks on download buttons), with no cookies and without identifying visitors.
  • RevenueCat: tracking business subscription status (account identifier and purchase identifier, with no bank details).

These providers only process data to run the service. Data exchanged with the app is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

How long we keep data

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your personal data is erased. Feedback you have sent is kept without any link to your identity.

Inactive accounts. A customer or business account that has not been used at all for 2 years is deleted along with its data. You are warned twice by email beforehand: just open the app to keep your account.

Businesses on the Free plan: with no sign-in and no validated customer visit for 3 months, the business is hidden from the map (warning by email one week before). It reappears as soon as you sign in again.

Subscription: see the subscription terms.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can access, correct or delete your data, object to its use or ask for it to be transferred. Write to us at fidorestocontact@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (cnil.fr).

To delete your account: see the dedicated page.

Children

FidOresto is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top of the page shows the latest version.
